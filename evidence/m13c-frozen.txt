Why this hash appears in the v0.0.1 release record
--------------------------------------------------

was placed behind #ifdef LUAPORT_SESSION_REUSE, a define that M13C does not
compile with.

M13C was therefore rebuilt after the fix and compared. It was byte-identical.

That is the proof — not the assertion, the measurement — that the Phase 2 edits
compiled out completely from every pre-session-reuse milestone, and that no
milestone other than M16C was disturbed by the FLASH64 work.
